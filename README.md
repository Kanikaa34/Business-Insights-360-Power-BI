# Business Insights 360 | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Business Insights 360 is a business intelligence project built using Power BI to analyze business performance across key functional areas. The project transforms business data into interactive dashboards that help stakeholders understand performance, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze financial performance and profitability.
- Evaluate sales trends and customer performance.
- Understand marketing performance and product metrics.
- Monitor supply chain performance and forecast accuracy.
- Develop interactive dashboards to support business decision-making.

## 📊 Dashboards

### 1. Finance View
Analyze financial performance through key metrics related to revenue, profitability, and other financial indicators included in the report.

### 2. Sales View
Explore sales performance, customer trends, and product-level metrics to identify growth opportunities.

### 3. Marketing View
Evaluate marketing performance and product metrics to understand business trends and performance.

### 4. Supply Chain View
Analyze supply chain performance and forecast-related metrics to identify potential operational improvements.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** — Interactive dashboards and data visualization
- **SQL (MySQL)** — Data querying, extraction, and analysis
- **DAX** — Measures, calculations, and business KPIs
- **DAX Studio** — DAX query analysis and performance investigation
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **Data Modeling** — Relationships between tables and analytical reporting
